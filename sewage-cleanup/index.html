Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. When it happens in Santa Fe, Santa Fe Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Our Sewage Cleanup Process

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle sewage cleanup from start to finish:

1

Safety Assessment

We start by measuring how far the sewage contamination reaches, noting any safety hazards and setting up containment so clean areas are not cross-contaminated.

2

Sewage Removal & Material Disposal

Standing sewage is pumped out completely. Carpet and its padding, insulation, drywall and other contaminated porous items are removed, then disposed of properly as biohazard waste.

3

Cleaning and Disinfecting

We thoroughly clean every surface that remains, then disinfect it with an EPA-registered antimicrobial solution. Meanwhile, air scrubbers fitted with HEPA filters pull pathogens and other contaminants from the air.

4

Drying & Odor Elimination

Commercial drying equipment removes all residual moisture. Sewage odors are eliminated through industrial deodorizing. The space is confirmed safe to occupy by clearance testing.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
