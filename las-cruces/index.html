Bursts: A pipe that has split is the most urgent call there is, because it puts out water for as long as the supply is on. Runoff: Storm water coming under a door or through a garage is next in line. There is no valve to close here, so lift what you can off the floor and avoid wet electrical outlets. Kitchens: Leaks from a dishwasher, an ice maker line or the plumbing under the kitchen sink should be dealt with the day you find them. Crawlspaces: Dampness in the space below the floor can wait a few days, though not a few months. Odors: A musty smell near the site of an old leak is the least urgent item here, yet it deserves an inspection soon.
