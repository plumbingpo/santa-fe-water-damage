Real remediation involves a great deal more than cleaning visible growth from a wall. When to Call for Mold Remediation in Santa Fe

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Closets, bathrooms or other damp, enclosed rooms that always smell musty
  • Peeling wallpaper, bubbling paint or warped materials that point to hidden moisture
  • Windows, cold surfaces or walls that sweat with condensation on a regular basis
  • Cleaning that never lasts, with growth returning to the same surface
  • Patches of mold, whatever the color, on a ceiling, wall, floor or other surface
  • Furniture against outside walls or items in closets developing speckled patches
  • A flood or leak in the recent past that never got a professional dry-out

Why Mold Remediation Is Not a DIY Job

Mold remediation is not a DIY project. Disturbing mold colonies without proper containment sends millions of spores airborne, which can contaminate unaffected areas of your home and create worse health exposure than the original problem. Our crews work in personal protective equipment inside a negative-pressure enclosure, with HEPA air scrubbers running, to trap spores and hold them in the work area while removal is underway.

Just as important, we deal with moisture, the underlying cause of every mold problem. If the moisture is left alone, taking away what you can see only buys time, and regrowth often shows up within weeks. Certified remediation technicians have the training and equipment to identify hidden moisture sources, dry affected areas properly and put solutions in place that prevent recurrence.

Mold Remediation Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Overflowing gutters send water into basements and crawl spaces, so clean them on a schedule
  • Laundry rooms, baths and kitchens all need working exhaust ventilation that is sized for the space
  • Address condensation on windows, pipes and cold surfaces promptly
  • Keep indoor humidity below 60% (ideally 30 to 50%) with dehumidifiers and proper ventilation
  • Repair any leak inside 24 hours, since 24 to 48 hours of moisture is all mold requires to begin
  • Mold in ductwork is prevented by servicing the heating and cooling system and replacing its filters on schedule
